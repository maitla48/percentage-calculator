# Privacy Policy — Percentage Calculator

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Percentage Calculator (`com.seotoolsarena.percentagecalculator`) is a free
offline calculator. We do not require an account, and we do not collect
your name, email, contacts, location, or calculation inputs.

## What stays on your device
- Calculations and history are stored only on your device (local storage).
- You can clear history inside the app at any time. Uninstalling the app
  deletes it.

## Advertising (AdMob)
The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob and its partners may
collect device identifiers, advertising IDs, and approximate location to
serve and measure ads, as described in Google's Privacy Policy
(https://policies.google.com/privacy) and AdMob's policies.
You can opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings
(Settings → Privacy → Ads → Delete advertising ID / Opt out of Ads
Personalisation), or via Google's ad settings.

We do not sell personal information. We do not knowingly collect data
from children under 13; the app is not directed at children.

## Third-party services
- Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) — advertising only.
- No analytics SDK, no crash reporting, no social SDKs are included.

## Data deletion
Because no personal data is sent to us, there is nothing to delete on a
server. Clear history in the app, or uninstall the app to remove all
local data.
